One-of-a-kind fabrication

Custom geosynthetic equipment built to spec.

When standard Grizzly rigs aren’t enough, we design and fabricate solutions that fit extreme widths, steep slopes, hover-head mounts, and specialty roll handling.

Engagement flow

1 · Discovery

Review roll specs, logistics, and integration targets.

2 · Design

Collaborative CAD and structural modeling.

3 · Fabrication

Heavy-duty builds staged for fit-up and QA.

4 · Commissioning

On-site startup, training, and lifecycle support.

Where custom builds make sense.

Landfill & stabilization rigs

Oversized Grizzly frames with PVC/roller extensions to cover ultra-wide cells.

Cable or rail-mounted heads

Hover-head assemblies that ride structural cables for steep slopes.

Multi-roll pole systems

Deploy multiple narrow rolls simultaneously for rapid trench or shoulder work.

Oil-truck integrations

Fully engineered rear decks that lift the laydown unit for one-pass oil + fabric.

How we collaborate.

Design sprints

Bring your drawings or start from a blank sheet—our team models structural loads, hydraulics, and mounting geometry.

Field validation

We stage builds for fit-up, then travel to site for commissioning, training, and future upgrade planning.

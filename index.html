Safer unloading

Protect workers. Protect the roll. Protect your bottom line.

Manual unloading is where crews get hurt and rolls get damaged. Roll Pullers eliminate the pry-bar-and-shoulder routine — one operator pulls the roll, guides it out, done.

  • Reduces back, shoulder, and hand injuries
  • Protects expensive rolls from crushed or torn cores
  • Multiple pullers can unload trucks up to 10× faster
  • Pays for itself in just a few shipments
Engineered right

Built for the pull, not the price point.

In 2016, after 37 years in the field, we re-engineered the original 12" RP4 into today's 16" XL platform and added the RP3 — for pulling heavier banded rolls without tearing.

  • Grabs 4" deeper into the cardboard core
  • Patent-pending laser-cut armor plate teeth
  • Forged ring, threaded and double-welded onto machined shaft
  • Handles multi-roll banded groups in one pull

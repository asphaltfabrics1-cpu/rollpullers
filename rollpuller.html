Workflow & best practices.

Recommended technique

  • Insert horizontally until the teeth seat, then lift slightly or pull level.
  • Guide rolls along rails, a dolly, or a waiting forklift to prevent core drag.
  • Never pull downward — downward angles add friction and can tear the core wall.
  • Stand by the side of the truck, not behind the ropes or chains.

Operational benefits

  • One operator unloads full containers in minutes.
  • Reduces shoulder and back injuries from prying rolls manually.
  • Protects expensive rolls from being rewound due to crushed cores.
  • Multiple pullers unload trucks and containers up to 10× faster.

Safety First

Roll Pullers don't break with proper use. Chains, cables, and ropes do.

Rules of the dock

  • Never exceed the Working Load Limit (WLL). WLL is lower than the breaking strength — that's the point.
  • Stand at the side of the truck, not behind or in line with the pull.
  • Do not stand by the ropes or chains. A snapped rigging line kills.
  • Watch OSHA regulations. Never assume common sense — train every operator.

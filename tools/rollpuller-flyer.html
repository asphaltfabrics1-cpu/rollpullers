Why Roll Pullers

Manual unloading of geosynthetic rolls from vans and containers is where crews get hurt and rolls get damaged. The Roll Puller™ eliminates the pry-bar-and-shoulder routine.

  • One operator pulls the roll — no team lift.
  • Up to 10× faster with multiple pullers.
  • Protects cardboard cores so rolls stay usable.
  • Pays for itself in a few shipments.

SAFETY FIRST

Roll Pullers do not break with proper use. Chains, cables, ropes, and slings do break.
1

Never exceed the Working Load Limit

The WLL is less than breaking strength — that's the point. OSHA requires you stay below it, every pull.

2

Stand to the side of the truck

Never stand behind the ropes, chains, or in line with the pull. A snapped rigging line kills.

3

Pull horizontally, not down

Insert the puller horizontally, then lift slightly or pull level. Downward pulls tear the core wall.

4

Train every operator

Speed and safety come from operators who understand the rig. Walk every new operator through the drill.

Recommended Technique

  • Insert horizontally until the teeth seat, then lift slightly or pull level.
  • Guide rolls along rails, a dolly, or a waiting forklift to prevent core drag.
  • For loosely wrapped rolls where cores start to pull out, use multiple Roll Pullers — one in each core — to distribute load.
  • Pyramided rolls can be pulled until roll weight exceeds the puller's tested capacity.
